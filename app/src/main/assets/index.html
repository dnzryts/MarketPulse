Breakout radar

How the score works (0–100): price at or near its 20-day high, today's volume against its 20-day average, trend (price above the 20- and 50-day averages), one-month momentum and RSI, then news buzz and headline tone over the past two days for the leaders. A high score means momentum and attention are lining up. It is not a forecast: breakouts often fail, so check the chart and news before acting. Not investment advice.

Stocks in these headlines

Headlines from Google News. Dots show headline tone: from keywords (like "beats", "plunges") until you run AI sentiment, which reads each headline and explains its likely impact. A guide only. Tap a story to open it in your browser.

Connect the AI analyst

The analyst uses Claude to research markets: it scans SGX, NASDAQ and NSE with the app's live data, reads the news, searches the web, and explains what it finds. Add your Anthropic API key to turn it on. Usage is billed to your Anthropic account.

Price alerts

Checked about every 15 minutes in the background, even when the app is closed (Android sets the timing). Price alerts switch off after they fire. Daily-move and chart-signal alerts (breakouts, RSI, golden/death cross, volume spikes) stay on and fire at most once a day.
